About

Engineering, with a design lens

I trained as a Computer Scientist and worked professionally as a full-stack C# developer before choosing to study Game Design — a deliberate step to pair that engineering foundation with a more creative, player-focused way of thinking about software.

My current interest sits at the intersection of AI systems and interactive design: how generative and constraint-based approaches can make games feel more responsive, and how to build that responsibly on top of solid engineering fundamentals rather than as a bolt-on.
